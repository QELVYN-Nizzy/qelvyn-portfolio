02 / About the designer

Distinctive visuals.
Intentional design.

I'm Nishith Gimhana, the designer behind NIZZY. I work with bold graphic compositions, expressive typography and illustration-led artwork, bringing a clear visual direction and careful attention to detail to each brief.

I enjoy bringing ideas together through contrast, layout, detail and color — creating graphics designed to catch the eye and hold attention.

I take on freelance design projects and creative collaborations. If you have a brief, a brand idea or a visual direction in mind, let's discuss the goals, deliverables and how to bring it to life.
